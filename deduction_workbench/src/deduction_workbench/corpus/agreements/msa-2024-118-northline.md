# Harbor & Field Master Supply Agreement

<!-- agreement_id: MSA-2024-118 -->
<!-- title: Harbor & Field Master Supply Agreement -->

Customer: Northline Grocery. Effective January 1, 2024. This agreement governs
invoice deductions taken by the customer against Harbor & Field shipments.

## §4.2 Shortage claims

<!-- id: MSA-2024-118 §4.2 -->
<!-- kind: shortage -->
<!-- terms: claim_window_days=15 -->
<!-- anchors: shortage short-ship proof of delivery pod quantity missing cases -->

A buyer may deduct for shortage only when the carrier proof of delivery, or our
ASN if the POD is silent, shows fewer saleable units than the invoice. The
deduction is limited to the shorted units at the invoice price. The claim must
reach us within 15 days of delivery. A deduction filed against a complete POD
is unauthorized and will be recovered.

## §5.1 Invoice price and promotions

<!-- id: MSA-2024-118 §5.1 -->
<!-- kind: pricing_default -->
<!-- terms: -->
<!-- anchors: invoice price promotion bill-back scan allowance authorized -->

The invoice price governs unless a separate written promotion or price
agreement covers the same SKU and the ship date. A bill-back, scan allowance,
or other price deduction taken without that agreement, outside its dates, above
its rate, or above its cap is unauthorized and will be recovered.

## §6.3 Returns

<!-- id: MSA-2024-118 §6.3 -->
<!-- kind: returns -->
<!-- terms: window_days=60 -->
<!-- anchors: return RMA authorization restock returned goods -->

A return deduction is allowed only when Harbor & Field issued an RMA before
the goods shipped back, the returned quantity does not exceed the RMA, and the
debit is at invoice price. The claim must be dated within 60 days of the
invoice. A return with no RMA is unauthorized and will be recovered.

## §7.4 Damaged goods

<!-- id: MSA-2024-118 §7.4 -->
<!-- kind: damaged_goods -->
<!-- terms: notice_days=10 -->
<!-- anchors: damaged goods concealed damage crushed carrier notice window -->

A damage deduction is allowed only for units that are crushed, leaking, or
otherwise unsaleable, supported by photographs or a carrier exception, and
limited to those units at invoice price. Notice must be given within 10 days
of delivery. A late or unsupported damage deduction is unauthorized and will
be recovered.

## §8.1 Trade funds

<!-- id: MSA-2024-118 §8.1 -->
<!-- kind: trade_funds -->
<!-- terms: -->
<!-- anchors: co-op advertising trade funds accrual separate written agreement -->

Co-op advertising and other trade funds may be deducted only under a separate
written agreement that is in force for that customer. Absent that agreement,
an advertising deduction is unauthorized and will be recovered.
