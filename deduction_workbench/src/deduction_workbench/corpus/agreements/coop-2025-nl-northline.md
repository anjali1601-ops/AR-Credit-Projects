## §2 Proof of performance

<!-- id: COOP-2025-NL §2 -->
<!-- kind: coop -->
<!-- terms: proof_window_days=45 -->
<!-- anchors: co-op advertising tear sheet proof of performance accrual COOP-2025-NL -->

Northline Grocery may deduct co-op advertising up to the accrued balance. The
advertisement must feature Harbor & Field brands. Proof of performance, a tear
sheet or a media invoice, must be submitted within 45 days of the ad run. A
deduction without proof, for creative that does not feature our brand, after
the proof window, or above the accrual is unauthorized and will be recovered.
