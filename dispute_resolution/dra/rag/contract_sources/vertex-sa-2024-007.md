### 3.3 Quantity Tolerance and Short Shipments

Supplier shall deliver the quantities stated on the accepted purchase order. No quantity
tolerance applies to this Agreement. Where the quantity recorded as shipped on Supplier's
packing list and shipping log is less than the quantity invoiced, the shortage is
established by Supplier's own records and Buyer need not provide further proof. Buyer
shall notify Supplier of any shortage within five (5) business days of delivery.

### 3.4 Remedies for Shortage

For an established shortage Buyer may elect, and Supplier shall provide, either (a)
delivery of the missing quantity within ten (10) business days at no additional freight
cost, or (b) a credit memorandum for the shortfall quantity at the unit price invoiced,
exclusive of tax. Where Buyer has short-paid the invoice by an amount not exceeding the
value of the established shortage, Supplier shall issue the credit memorandum and treat
the invoice as settled in full.

### 4.5 Pricing, Volume Rebates and Price Protection

Unit prices are fixed for each contract year. Volume rebates under an executed rebate
schedule accrue quarterly and are settled by credit memorandum after the quarter closes.
Rebates may not be taken as a deduction from a current invoice.

### 10.1 Dispute Escalation

The parties shall attempt to resolve any invoice dispute at the working level within
fifteen (15) days. Failing resolution, the dispute is escalated to the parties' finance
leads for a further fifteen (15) days before any formal proceeding is commenced. Amounts
not in dispute shall be paid when due notwithstanding any dispute over other amounts.
