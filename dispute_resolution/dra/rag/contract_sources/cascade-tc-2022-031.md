### 6.1 Payment Terms

Invoices are payable net forty-five (45) days from the invoice date. Payment is deemed
made on the date cleared funds are received by Supplier, not on the date the payment file
is created or mailed.

### 6.4 Early Payment Discount

Where an executed discount schedule is in force, Buyer may take the early payment
discount stated in that schedule only if cleared funds for the full invoice amount are
received by Supplier on or before the tenth (10th) day following the invoice date. The
discount is earned by the timing of payment and by nothing else. A discount taken on a
payment received after the tenth day, or taken after the discount schedule has expired or
been terminated, is not earned, and the amount of the discount remains immediately due.
Partial payment does not earn a proportionate discount.

### 6.5 Unauthorized Deductions and Chargebacks

Any deduction, chargeback or short payment that is not supported by a credit memorandum
issued by Supplier and referenced by number on the remittance advice is unauthorized.
Supplier will notify Buyer of an unauthorized deduction, and Buyer shall remit the
deducted amount within fifteen (15) days of that notice. Repeated unauthorized deductions
entitle Supplier to suspend the discount schedule on thirty (30) days' notice.

### 8.2 Returns and Product Complaints

Product may be returned only under a return authorization issued by Supplier. Requests
must be made within seven (7) days of delivery and must identify the lot number and the
nature of the complaint. Temperature-sensitive product for which the cold-chain record is
unbroken is not eligible for return on grounds of suspected excursion.
