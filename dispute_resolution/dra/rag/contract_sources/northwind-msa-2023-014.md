### 4.1 Prices and Price Changes

Prices are those set out in the then-current Supplier price list as modified by any
written discount agreement executed by both parties. Supplier may amend list prices on
thirty (30) days' written notice. Orders accepted before the effective date of a price
change are invoiced at the prior price. Buyer's purchase order terms do not vary this
Agreement.

### 5.2 Title and Risk of Loss

All shipments are made FOB Supplier's dock. Title and risk of loss pass to Buyer when the
goods are tendered to the carrier. Where the carrier's proof of delivery records the
goods as received in apparent good order, the goods are presumed to have been delivered
conforming, and the burden of establishing non-conformity rests with Buyer.

### 7.1 Inspection and Acceptance

Buyer shall inspect all goods promptly on delivery. Goods not rejected in writing within
ten (10) days of the delivery date recorded on the carrier's proof of delivery are deemed
accepted for all purposes, including for the calculation of amounts due.

### 7.3 Damaged Goods and Concealed Damage Claims

Where goods are damaged in transit, Buyer shall (a) cause the damage to be noted on the
carrier's proof of delivery at the time of receipt or, in the case of concealed damage,
notify Supplier in writing within ten (10) days of the delivery date, and (b) retain the
affected goods and packaging for inspection. A claim supported by a proof of delivery
annotated with the damage, or by a carrier exception report, and submitted within the
notice period, shall be settled by credit memorandum for the invoiced value of the
damaged units. Claims submitted after the notice period, or unsupported by a delivery
annotation or carrier exception report, are not allowable and may not be deducted from
amounts otherwise due.

### 7.4 Credit Memoranda

Approved claims are settled exclusively by credit memorandum issued against the original
invoice within fifteen (15) business days of approval. Credit memoranda are applied to
the invoice they reference and may not be netted against unrelated balances.

### 9.2 Deductions and Set-Off

Buyer shall pay each invoice in full by its due date. Buyer may not deduct, offset or
withhold any amount from an invoice except (a) against a credit memorandum previously
issued by Supplier and identified by number on the remittance advice, or (b) where
Supplier has agreed the deduction in writing. Any other deduction is an unauthorized
deduction and remains due and payable, together with any late-payment charges accruing
under clause 6.6 from the original due date.
